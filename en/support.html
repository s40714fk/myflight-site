Adding flights

  • Screenshots: Take a screenshot of the booking details in an airline’s app or website, then share it to “My Flight” or choose it with + → Read from Screenshots.
  • Wallet boarding passes: Open the boarding pass in Wallet and share it to My Flight.
  • Booking emails: Select and share the email text, or copy it and paste it in + → Read from Copied Text.
  • E-ticket PDFs: Open the PDF in Files or another app and share it, or choose it with + → Read from PDF.
  • Manually: Use + → Enter Manually to type the flight number, date and more.

You can check and correct the results before adding them. Text recognition happens on your device.

Importing booking emails automatically

With a personal automation in the Shortcuts app, flights can be added automatically when a booking email arrives.

  1. In My Flight, open … → Email Import and enter your name as it appears in emails, plus your family members’ names.
  2. In Shortcuts → Automation, choose Email and enter the airline’s booking email addresses under Sender.
  3. Add the action “Import Flights from Email” (My Flight) and pass the Email variable’s Content as Text.

Flights are added only from emails that contain your name or a registered family member’s name. Cancellation emails move the flight to Canceled Flights.

FAQ

The arrival time or other fields are empty.
Fields that aren’t in the text you passed stay empty. Open the flight and choose … → Edit to fill them in. If you have a FlightAware AeroAPI key, enter it in … → Flight Status to look up empty fields by flight number and date (optional).

I want my family to see my flights.
Invite them with … → Share with Family, which uses iCloud sharing. Invited people can only view your flights; they can’t edit or delete them.

What do the airline website buttons do?
They open the airline’s booking page inside the app. If you enter your booking name in Me & Family, the page opens with your name and booking reference filled in. Use of the airline’s website is subject to the airline’s terms.

Where is my passport number stored?
Only in this iPhone’s keychain; it is never sent to iCloud. Face ID is required to view it.

Is this an airline’s app?
No. My Flight is developed independently and is not affiliated with any airline.
