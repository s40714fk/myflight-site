Adding flights

  • Screenshots: Take a screenshot of the booking details in an airline’s app or website, then share it to “My Flight” or choose it with + → Read from Screenshots.
  • Wallet boarding passes: Open the boarding pass in Wallet and share it to My Flight.
  • Booking emails: Select and share the email text, or copy it and paste it in + → Read from Copied Text.
  • E-ticket PDFs: Open the PDF in Files or another app and share it, or choose it with + → Read from PDF.
  • Manually: Use + → Enter Manually to type the flight number, date and more.

You can check and correct the results before adding them. Text recognition happens on your device.

Shinkansen, hotels and rental cars

  • Shinkansen and limited express: Share or paste booking emails from EX (smartEX) and Ekinet just like flight emails, and the trains appear in the same list as your flights. Change emails update the train, and refund emails move it to Canceled Flights. Because these emails don’t include passenger names, choose the passengers and each person’s seat with Edit on the train.
  • Hotels and rental cars: In the Hotels or Rental Cars tab at the bottom, tap + and paste the booking email. Toyota Rent a Car and hotel booking emails from tripla, Booking.com, Jalan, Rakuten Travel, Ikyu and others are supported. Cancellation emails move the booking to Canceled Bookings.
  • View, Change or Cancel Booking opens the booking page from the email. When you paste text copied in the Mail app, the link URLs are read too. The cancellation policy is shown as a table.
  • Booking page URLs and cancellation policies stay on this iPhone and are not sent when sharing with family.

Importing booking emails automatically

With a personal automation in the Shortcuts app, flights can be added automatically when a booking email arrives.

  1. In My Flight, open … → Email Import and enter your name as it appears in emails, plus your family members’ names.
  2. In Shortcuts → Automation, choose Email and enter the booking email addresses of airlines and others under Sender (train, hotel and rental car booking emails can be imported the same way).
  3. Add the action “Import Flights from Email” (My Flight) and pass the Email variable’s Content as Text.

Flights are added only from emails that contain your name or a registered family member’s name. Cancellation emails move the flight to Canceled Flights.

FAQ

The arrival time or other fields are empty.
Fields that aren’t in the text you passed stay empty. Open the flight and choose … → Edit to fill them in. If you have a FlightAware AeroAPI key, enter it in … → Flight Status to look up empty fields by flight number and date (optional).

I want my family to see my flights.
Invite them with … → Share with Family, which uses iCloud sharing. Invited people can only view your flights; they can’t edit or delete them.

What do the airline website buttons do?
They open the airline’s booking page inside the app. If you enter your booking name in Me & Family, the page opens with your name and booking reference filled in. Use of the airline’s website is subject to the airline’s terms.

Where is my passport number stored?
Only in this iPhone’s keychain; it is never sent to iCloud. Face ID is required to view it.

Is there a widget?
Yes. Widgets for the Home Screen (small and medium) and the Lock Screen show your next flight. Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen and choose Edit to add one. Your own flights are shown first, and tapping a flight opens it in the app.

A payment deadline is shown.
When you import a booking e-mail sent before payment (ANA’s holding deadline or JAL’s purchase deadline), the deadline appears in the list and on the flight, and you get reminders 1 day and 3 hours before it. It disappears when you import the purchase e-mail or a ticket number is added, or when you tap “Paid (clear deadline)” on the flight. If the deadline passes without payment, the airline cancels the booking, so the flight moves to Canceled Flights (ANA doesn’t send an e-mail for this).

I changed my booking.
When you import a booking change e-mail (JAL’s “before change” and “after change”), the people who changed are removed from the old flight, and the new flight is added with the new booking reference.

Can each family member have a color?
Open a person in … → Me & Family and choose a color. The name tags in the list use that color. Colors are also kept in your iCloud together with names and other details.

Do canceled and past flights stay?
Flights you’ve taken stay in Past forever. Canceled flights are removed 1 week after they were canceled.

My flights disappeared after reinstalling the app.
The names and details of you and your family come back automatically from your iCloud. Flights, hotels and rental cars that were sent to iCloud with Share with Family can be brought back with Restore My Bookings from iCloud in Share with Family (boarding passes, payment deadlines, booking page URLs and cancellation policies are not restored).

Can I use it on iPhone Duo or a larger screen?
Yes. On wide screens such as an unfolded iPhone Duo, the list is shown on the left and the flight on the right. Landscape is supported too.

Is this an airline’s app?
No. My Flight is developed independently and is not affiliated with any airline, railway, hotel or rental car company.
